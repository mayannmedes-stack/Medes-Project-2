# KCC landing-page research notes

This project uses public information checked against Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.'s official website on 2026-11-20.

- Homepage: https://kcc.edu.ph/
  - KCC describes itself as a Christ-centered Catholic educational institution in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental.
  - Public footer details: Guanzon St., Brgy. 1, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental; (032) 4712-479; kcc_1927@yahoo.com; office hours 8:00 AM–12:NN and 1:00–5:00 PM.
  - Motto: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Mt 28:19.
- About page: https://kcc.edu.ph/about
  - School philosophy centers on forming socially conscious, responsible, and service-oriented individuals guided by Christ's teachings.
  - Vision and mission wording is summarized in the hero/about sections using short, attribution-safe copy.
  - History identifies the founding as Kabankalan Institute in 1927.
  - The official seal page describes dark blue and gold as symbols connected with knowledge, moral strength, eternal light, truth, and honor.
  - Core-value acronym listed by KCC: KCC IANS — Kindness, Christ-Centeredness, Creativity, Integrity, Accountability, Nurturing, Synodality.
- Programs page: https://kcc.edu.ph/programs
  - College groupings used in the cards: College of Management, Accountancy and Technology; College of Education, Arts and Sciences; College of Criminology.
  - Highlighted programs include BSBA, BSIT, BSA, BSAIS, BSMA, BSTM, BEEd, BSEd, BECEd, BPEd, AB Philosophy, BS Psychology, and BS Criminology.
- Admissions page: https://kcc.edu.ph/admissions
  - Four steps: Submit Requirements, Entrance Examination, Interview, Enrollment.
  - Requirements include a completed application form, Report Card / Form 138, Certificate of Good Moral Character, PSA Birth Certificate, 2x2 ID photos, and Honorable Dismissal for transferees.
- Contact page: https://kcc.edu.ph/contact
  - Reconfirms the public address, phone, email, and office hours used in the footer and contact strip.

Design note: the page is an assignment prototype, not an official KCC website. The CTA links to the public KCC admissions page and the footer includes an official-site link for verification.
